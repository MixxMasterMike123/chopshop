## 1. Background and structure

1.1 Meteor operates an e-commerce platform on which independent merchants ("**Merchants**") sell goods to consumers. Each Merchant is the **controller** of its customers' personal data; Meteor processes that data as the Merchant's **processor** under a data processing agreement between Meteor and each Merchant.

1.2 Meteor engages Snapwear to produce (print) and ship print-on-demand goods ordered by Merchants' customers. In doing so, Snapwear processes customers' personal data on Meteor's behalf and is a **sub-processor** under Article 28(4) GDPR.

1.3 This Agreement therefore establishes the Article 28(3)–(4) GDPR terms between Meteor (Processor) and Snapwear (Sub-processor). References in the Clauses to the "controller" shall, as between the Parties, be read as references to Meteor acting on behalf of its Controllers, and references to the "processor" as references to Snapwear, unless the context requires otherwise.

## 2. Incorporation of the Standard Contractual Clauses

2.1 The Parties agree to be bound by the **Standard Contractual Clauses between controllers and processors** adopted by the European Commission in **Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/915 of 4 June 2021** (the "**Clauses**"), the full text of which is available at https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2021/915/oj and is attached as **Schedule 1**. The Clauses are incorporated into this Agreement and, in accordance with Clause 4 of the Clauses, prevail over any conflicting provision of any other agreement between the Parties.

2.2 The options and specifications the Clauses leave to the Parties are set out in Section 3 below. The Annexes required by the Clauses are set out in Annexes I–IV of this Agreement.

## 4. Additional undertakings

4.1 **Purpose limitation.** Snapwear shall not use personal data or artwork received from Meteor for any purpose other than fulfilling the specific order, including not for marketing, profiling, analytics beyond service operation, or training of any automated system.

4.2 **No contact with data subjects.** Snapwear shall not contact Merchants' customers directly, except as required by law or expressly instructed by Meteor in writing. All customer communication runs through Meteor and the Merchant.

4.3 **Shipping labels and packaging.** Snapwear shall not display its own or Print Logistic's name to the recipient other than as required by carrier and customs regulations, as agreed commercially between the Parties.

4.4 **Confidentiality.** Snapwear shall ensure that every person authorised to process personal data is bound by a contractual or statutory obligation of confidentiality.

4.5 **Records.** Each Party maintains the records required by Article 30 GDPR.

## 5. Term

This Agreement enters into force on the date of the last signature below and remains in force for as long as Snapwear processes personal data on Meteor's behalf.

# ANNEX II — DESCRIPTION OF THE PROCESSING

**Categories of data subjects:** consumers who purchase goods from Merchants' shops on the Meteor platform (end customers); occasionally Merchants' staff whose contact details appear on an order.

**Categories of personal data:**
- Identification and contact data: first name, last name, email address, telephone number.
- Delivery data: postal address (street, apartment, postcode, city, country).
- Order data: ordered products, sizes, colours, quantities, Meteor order reference (`job_id`), shipping method, tracking number.
- Content data: artwork and mockup files supplied for printing, which may incidentally contain personal data (e.g. names, images of persons) placed there by the Merchant.
- Communications relating to claims, reprints and delivery problems.

**Sensitive data:** none is intended to be processed. Should artwork incidentally contain special-category data, Snapwear processes it solely to print the ordered item and applies the restrictions in Clause 7.5.

**Nature of the processing:** receipt and storage of order and address data via API or merchant panel; retrieval and storage of artwork/mockup files; printing; packing; generation of shipping labels; handover to carriers; storage of tracking data; handling of claims and reprints; deletion.

**Purpose:** production and delivery of print-on-demand goods ordered by Merchants' customers through the Meteor platform, and the handling of related claims and reprints.

**Duration:** for the term of the Agreement; per-order data is retained no longer than 90 days after delivery (see Section 3, Clause 7.3), subject to legal retention obligations.

**Frequency:** continuous (per order).

**For processing by sub-processors:** as described in Annex IV; the same categories of data, limited to what each sub-processor needs for its function (e.g. carriers receive name, address, telephone and email for delivery only).
