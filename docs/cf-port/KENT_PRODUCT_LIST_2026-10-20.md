## Against what is built (2026-10-20)

- The Prodigi catalogue of 2026-10-19 holds 64000, 5000, 2000, 64V00, STTU169 (as TEE-SS-STTU755), 18500, JH001, JH030, BC3001. Of Kent's NOW garments only 64000 and 18500 are in it; 18600 and 18000 must be added (18000 answered no SKU in the sandbox on 2026-10-19: find the right SKU string). 5000, 2000, 64V00, STTU169, JH001, JH030, BC3001 are in the catalogue but not on Kent's list.
- Every non-apparel NOW item (postcards, stickers, framed posters) is a new product class: one flat print area, no garment, no colour/size attributes in the same sense, a different studio (no mockup on a body), and several ship only from the UK (customs/shipping to SE differ from the PL/NL labs). None of the POD code (print spec, DTG profile, canvas, mapping) covers them today.
